# 🏥 Medical Treatment Process During Winter/Summer Breaks Away from Zhuhai

::: danger Notice
This process is for BNBU students who need to receive medical treatment outside of Zhuhai. Please follow the steps below to complete the "Medical Treatment Away from Place of Insurance" registration (异地就医备案).
:::

## Pre-requisites

If choosing online operation, you can add **BNBU Medical and Health Education Center** WeChat: **a13928025919**
If choosing offline operation, please go to **BNBU Medical Center** to pick up the forms.

## Step 1: Designating a Primary Medical Institution

Complete the outpatient selection registration in WeChat to set the school medical center as your designated medical institution.

### Steps for Selection

1. Open **WeChat Mini Program**
2. Search and enter **Zhuhai Social Security Mobile Office** (珠海社保掌上办)
3. Select **Medical and Maternity Insurance** (医疗生育保险)
4. Click **Outpatient Selection Registration** (门诊选点登记)
5. Select **Add New Selection** (新增选点)
6. Select **Outpatient Pooling Selection** (门诊统筹选点)
7. Select Area: **High-tech Industrial Development Zone** (高新技术产业开发区)
8. Select Institution: **BNBU Medical Center** (北师香港浸会大学医务室)

## Step 2: Obtaining and Filling Out the Registration Form

1. Obtain the **Medical Treatment Away from Place of Insurance Registration Form** (异地就医备案表) online via WeChat or offline.
2. Fill out the form truthfully.
3. Return the completed form to the **Medical and Health Education Center for stamping**.

:::info Note
The stamping process usually takes 3-5 working days. Please be patient. The medical center will notify you to collect the form after stamping.
:::

## Step 3: Applying for "Medical Treatment Away from Place of Insurance"

Complete the application in the WeChat Mini Program.

### Application Steps

1. Open **WeChat Mini Program**
2. Search and enter **Zhuhai Social Security Mobile Office** (珠海社保掌上办)
3. Select **Medical and Maternity Insurance** (医疗生育保险)
4. Click **Medical Treatment Away from Place of Insurance Registration** (异地就医备案)
5. Enter **Phone Number**
6. Select Registration Type: **Long-term Residence Away from Place of Insurance (including university students)** (异地长期居住（含在校大学生）)
7. Enter Place of Treatment: **Destination Address** (the city/hospital where you will receive treatment)
8. Enter Place of Insurance: **School Address**
9. Select Material Type: **Long-term Residence Certification Type** (长期居住认定类型)
10. Click **Next**
11. **Take a photo and upload** the stamped registration form from Step 2.

::: info
Approval usually takes within 10 minutes.
:::

## FAQ

### Q: How long does it take for the registration to be approved?

A: Generally, it takes 1-3 working days to complete the review after submission.

### Q: Why is "Medical Treatment Away from Place of Insurance" registration required?

China's **Basic Medical Insurance** adopts a location-based management system, meaning the insured person's medical insurance relationship is bound to the place of insurance (Zhuhai). When you need to receive medical treatment in a city other than Zhuhai, it is considered "Medical Treatment Away from Place of Insurance".

Without registration, you cannot settle expenses directly at designated medical institutions outside the insurance location by swiping the medical insurance card/code. You must **pay all medical expenses out of pocket first**, and then apply for manual reimbursement in Zhuhai.

Unregistered out-of-town medical treatment usually results in a **significant reduction in reimbursement ratio**, increasing the personal burden. Additionally, you must collect and mail back a large number of invoices and medical records, leading to a long approval cycle.

### Q: Benefits of Registration

- **Direct Settlement**: You can settle expenses directly at designated medical institutions outside the insurance location by swiping the medical insurance card/code, only paying the personal co-payment portion.
- **Normal Reimbursement Ratio**: It follows the "Place of Treatment Catalog, Place of Insurance Policy" rule, meaning drugs and diagnostic items follow the treatment location's regulations, and the reimbursement ratio follows Zhuhai's policy normally.

### Q: How to use

Open the "My Medical Insurance Certificate" WeChat Mini Program, inform the staff and show the QR code when paying; or show the social security card.
